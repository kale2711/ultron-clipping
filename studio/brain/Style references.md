# Style references (the owner's standard for our videos)

Reference videos the owner says are "what we're looking for". These override the generic playbook.
For each new reference: `python3 studio/studio.py ref <file>`, look at `sheet.jpg` and `first_2s.jpg`, read the
transcript, then add an entry below under its topic. When several references agree, promote the common thread to
"House standard" and bake it into the matching preset in `studio/presets/`.

Record for each reference: length, shape (full-frame / letterboxed / split), hook (first 2s: what we see + hear),
cut pace (avg shot length), captions (font feel, size, colour, position, word-by-word or sentences, emoji),
text cards, voice (talking / voiceover / none), music, colour/look, transitions/effects, ending (CTA / loop), and
WHY it works.
