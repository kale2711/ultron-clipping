What the app accesses

When a TikTok account is connected, ultronclipping receives through TikTok's official API:

  • basic profile information (account identifier, display name, avatar);
  • account statistics (follower, like and video counts);
  • the list of the account's public videos with their view, like and share counts;
  • permission to publish videos to the account.

How it is used

Only to publish the account holder's videos and to show the account's statistics to the account holder on a private dashboard.

Storage

Access tokens and statistics are stored on the operator's own computer or private server. They are not stored with any third party other than the hosting of that server.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising. It is disclosed only if the law requires it.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account stays connected. Disconnect at any time in TikTok under Settings, Security and permissions, Apps and services; tokens then stop working. To have stored data deleted, use the contact link below and it will be deleted within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at anyone under 18.
